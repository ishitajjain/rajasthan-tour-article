My Recommendation (for a Rajasthan Tour Package)

If it’s your first time in Jaipur, I’d lean toward Old City / Pink City or C-Scheme / MI Road. You’ll be close to major attractions, and the atmosphere is very “Jaipur.”

If you prefer heritage and to feel like royalty, stay closer to Amber Fort. Especially nice if you’re doing a tour that includes forts, palaces, and countryside — you get a more immersive experience.

If you want peace + good transport, Bani Park is a solid option — quieter but accessible.

If your priority is local life + food, Raja Park is fun and authentic.

Tips When Booking for a Tour Package

Ask your tour operator if they have preferred hotels in any of these areas — they often negotiate better rates.

Check how far your hotel is from key Jaipur attractions — even on a tour, you may have some free time to explore.

Look at transport logistics: for example, getting to/from Amber Fort is easier if you stay nearby.

Check reviews of hotels for things like safety, cleanliness, and service, especially if you're staying in heritage properties.

For heritage hotels, book early — they can fill up quickly, especially during peak tourist season.
